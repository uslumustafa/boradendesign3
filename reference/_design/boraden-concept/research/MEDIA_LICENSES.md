# Media and thought-source register

## Generated images

53 separate built-in ImageGen jobs, one for each of 47 service/learning areas and six sectors. All completed. Stored under `assets/services/` and `assets/sectors/`; JPEG conversion preserves the generated composition. Original prompts and individual video shot descriptions: `media-plan.json`. Output paths and original PNG provenance: `generated-media.json`.

The existing home/About images remain as previously generated. All corporate scene images are illustrative, not real employees or client photographs. No company logos, financial figures or project outcomes are inferred from these images.

25 additional selected images now cover general pages, service categories, individual calculators, four article covers and a separate About second scene. Four outputs were intentionally refined for subject accuracy. Files: `assets/pages/`. Original prompts and per-page video briefs: `page-media-plan.json`; selected originals, refinement prompts and hashes: `page-media-generated.json`. The shipped 83 public routes have 83 different primary-image file hashes, including the three authentic thinker portraits.
